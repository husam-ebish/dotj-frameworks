Compatible hex/binary flag combinations therefore validate but parse decimal digits, rather than changing the radix. The selected facade preserves this observed bounded behavior by validating integer-style combinations and projecting remaining whitespace flags into its decimal parser. This is not a general hex/binary Decimal feature claim. Native invalid-combination messages are compared exactly. Decimal and NumberFormatInfo archive hashes bind this behavior.
