## Provenance process for later work

Record upstream path/revision, license file/version, copied or adapted region, test/asset provenance, modifications, contributor attribution and generated-code inputs. Keep required notices in each artifact and source distribution. Track dependency conditions and binary/native assets in an SBOM. Preserve source eligibility decisions when upgrading upstream. Do not use original logos or imply endorsement.

Where terms are unsuitable or unclear, retain the target and mark the affected work blocked. Possible decisions include selecting a verified eligible historical baseline, obtaining suitable rights, or changing the derivation approach through an explicit project decision. This plan does not silently choose a workaround or give legal advice.
